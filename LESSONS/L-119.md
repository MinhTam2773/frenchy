- `L-119 · 2026-10-18 · tooling · T-28` — **After a restart, `pkill -f "npm ci"` meant to stop my own duplicate installs also matched my shell's command line (it killed the shell) and could have hit other agents' installs, since every agent shares the box; two `npm ci` runs in one folder had also been started by a retried command.** → Never `pkill -f` by pattern here; find the pid whose `/proc/<pid>/cwd` is your worktree and kill that one, or just wait. Start `npm ci` once, in the foreground, and retry only after it has exited.
